Piano roll

  • Click an empty spot to add a note, and keep dragging to place it
  • Drag a note to move it, or drag its right edge to change its length
  • Right-click a note to delete it. Right-drag deletes several
  • Ctrl+drag draws a selection box. Shift+click adds or removes a note from the selection
  • Double-click a note to select every note at that pitch
  • Drag in the VEL lane to set how hard notes are played: harder is louder and brighter. Shift+drag draws a straight line, for a crescendo or a fade
  • Select notes and press Slide to make them glide in from the note before, like an acid bass line. Glide in the Shape row sets how long the slide takes
  • Hold Alt while dragging to ignore the snap grid
  • Ghosts shows what the other tracks play at the same point in the song, faintly and in their colours, so a bass line or melody fits the chords. It follows the playhead in Song mode; click the playlist ruler to look at another spot
  • Draw picks what a click places: a note, or a triad, seventh, power chord or octave, built in the song’s key
  • Movement makes a sound move with the beat: Wobble sweeps the filter (great on bass), Tremolo pulses the volume, Auto-pan swings it left and right. Speed is a note length, so it always stays on the tempo
  • Pick Sampler as the sound to play your own sound: load any audio file or press Record a sound and sing one note. BrowserDJ finds its pitch, so every key plays it in tune
  • Chords… fills the pattern with a chord progression in the song’s key, one chord per bar (shorter patterns get quicker changes). On a Bass or Acid track it writes the root notes instead, so one progression gives you both parts. Change the Key first to hear it elsewhere
  • Arpeggiate turns chords into running notes on the snap grid (up, down, up-down). Humanize loosens velocities a little
  • Click the top ruler to choose where playback starts

Keys

  • Space play or stop   Ctrl+Z undo   Ctrl+Shift+Z redo
  • ↑ ↓ transpose (Shift for an octave)   ← → nudge
  • Delete remove   Ctrl+A select all   Esc deselect
  • Ctrl+C / X / V copy, cut, paste at the marker   Ctrl+D duplicate
  • Q quantize (when “Keyboard plays” is off)
  • Wheel scroll   Shift+wheel scroll sideways   Ctrl+wheel zoom   Alt+wheel row height   Middle-drag pan

Playing live

  • Record with Melody picked: hum or sing, and it becomes notes. With Drums picked: beatbox, and it becomes a drum pattern (“boots” = kick, “ka” or “psh” = snare, “ts” = hi-hat). Load a recording works both ways too
  • With Keyboard plays on, Z–M (with S, D, G, H, J as the black keys) and Q–U (with 2, 3, 5, 6, 7) play two octaves of the selected track. Shift plays louder
  • On a drum track the same keys play the kit, one drum per key from Z
  • Connect MIDI keyboard to play from a real keyboard or pad controller (Chrome and Edge)
  • Turn on Record notes and press Space: what you play goes into the pattern while it loops, snapped to the grid. Stopping ends the take; one undo removes it

Playlist

  • Click a lane to place that track’s current pattern. Click a clip to edit its pattern below
  • Drag a clip to move it, drag its right edge to repeat the pattern
  • Ctrl+drag draws a selection box (Shift adds to it). Dragging a selected clip moves them all; Shift+drag copies. Right-click deletes a clip
  • On the selection: Delete, Ctrl+D duplicate after it, Ctrl+C / X / V copy, cut, paste at the marker, Ctrl+A all, Esc none
  • Make unique (or U when “Keyboard plays” is off): the selected clips get their own copy of the pattern, to make a variation in just that spot
  • Double-click a clip to split it in two at that bar (or press Split / K to cut at the song marker). Drag a clip’s left edge to trim its start: the music stays where it is
  • Clips snap to bars. Hold Alt to snap to beats
  • Clips are links: change the pattern once and every clip of it changes. Use Clone to make a variation
  • Click the playlist’s ruler to play the song from there, the piano roll’s ruler to loop the pattern
  • Drag along the ruler to make a loop region; drag its edges to change it, right-click it to remove it
  • Press A on a track to open its automation lane. Pick a setting (volume, filter, pan, drive, echo), click to add points, drag them, right-click to delete. Automation plays in Song mode
  • Tempo (top left of the playlist) opens the tempo lane: click a bar and type a tempo to change it from there on. Drag a change up or down to set it, sideways to move it; right-click removes it. Tempo changes play in Song mode, go into WAV and MIDI files, and come in from MIDI files
  • Double-click the ruler to add a marker. Click a marker to jump there, drag to move it, right-click to delete it

Drums

  • Click or drag across steps to turn them on or off
  • Right-click a step to make it an accent or a soft ghost hit
  • Kit picks how the drums sound: Classic, 808 (booming kick, snappy snare), Acoustic or Lo-fi (crunchy and dark)
  • Turn on Rolls and click a step to split it into 2, 3 or 4 quick hits, for hi-hat rolls and snare build-ups
  • Click a name to hear the sound. Drop an audio file on a row (or use its ↥ button) to use your own; × goes back to the built-in sound
  • Your own sounds are kept in this browser, and Save puts them inside the project file so it opens complete anywhere

Audio tracks

  • + Audio track holds real recordings: your voice, a guitar, a loop
  • Put the song marker where you want to start and press Record over the song: one bar of clicks, then the song plays and you sing or play along. Wear headphones so the microphone only hears you
  • Load audio file (or drop one while the audio track is selected) brings in a WAV, MP3, M4A, OGG or FLAC
  • Each recording is a take; its clip plays it once. Drag the edges of the waveform to trim (the sound stays where it is in the song), and set the level and effects in the mixer

Songs and files

  • My songs lists everything you have worked on in this browser: open, rename or delete
  • Open takes a BrowserDJ project or a MIDI file (from FL Studio or anywhere); you can also drop either on the page
  • Share makes a link with the whole song inside it; Save keeps a file, with your own sounds included
  • Works offline after your first visit. Install app (Chrome, Edge, Android) puts it on your desktop or home screen; on iPhone use Share, then Add to Home Screen
Everything runs in this page. Your recording never leaves your computer, and your project is saved in this browser. For the cleanest notes, use headphones or a quiet room and keep the microphone close.
